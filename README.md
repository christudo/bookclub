Project Title: Bookclub

Project Description: The project is a dedicated online platform created in honor of World Book Day, aimed at promoting awareness and understanding of the experiences of asylum seekers through a curated database of recommended books. This user-friendly web application allows visitors to explore a selection of stories, organized by the year of recommendation. While registration is optional, registered users can personalize their experience by tracking their reading lists, including books they’ve read, are currently reading, or wish to read in the future. The project not only enhances the visibility of literature championed by a nonprofit organization advocating for refugees but also encourages a broader conversation about empathy and understanding through reading.

Methods: The website features an engaging overview page displaying book cover thumbnails that link to individual book pages. Each book page provides essential information such as the title, author, and a brief description, along with easy access to further resources via WorldCat, helping users locate these books at local libraries or purchase them from various vendors. The design prioritizes a clean and consistent aesthetic, ensuring that the stories of the asylum seekers take center stage. By offering an optional login for users, the platform fosters a sense of ownership and engagement while also providing the opportunity to create personalized reading lists tailored to individual preferences.

I faced a technical challenge while migrating my code from a development environment, specifically Visual Studio Code, to a web hosting platform, Render. This transition required a solid understanding of both the coding and processes involved in creating a functional web application. Moving from a local development environment to a production environment required careful management of dependencies and ensuring that the syntax and structure of my code complied with the hosting platform's standards. Keeping detailed notes about the coding process and the specifics of my database proved to be a helpful strategy. Overall, this project demanded a combination of coding skills, critical thinking, problem-solving, and a willingness to learn and adapt to new environments and technologies.

My Role: As the creator of the website, my primary goal was to design an intuitive and user-friendly experience that ensures easy navigation and maintains a clean, consistent aesthetic across all pages. I aimed to highlight the stories within each book, allowing them to take center stage without distractions. To enhance accessibility, I've linked each book to WorldCat, enabling users to locate them at local libraries or purchase them from various vendors. Additionally, to accommodate new users, I've implemented a feature that allows them to create personalized book lists on our platform. This functionality lets users track the books they want to read, those they are currently reading, or those they have completed, creating a tailored reading experience.

Learning Outcome: Technology

Rationale: The primary motivation behind establishing a book club is to foster a sense of community and connection among readers while promoting meaningful conversations around literature. By creating a platform dedicated to discussing selected books, I aim to enhance the reading experience and encourage members to explore different narratives, particularly those that focus on important social issues such as the rights of refugees. Highlighting books curated by a nonprofit organization advocating for refugee rights will not only provide valuable insights into their stories but also bring awareness to these pressing matters. 

This approach ensures that the selection is not only engaging but also impactful, allowing members to connect literature with real-world issues. To improve user experience and participation, I have implemented an optional log-in feature that permits members to interact with the platform at their convenience. Those who choose to sign up as verified users can create personalized reading lists. This functionality fosters a tailored reading experience that aligns with individual interests and promotes deeper engagement within the Bookclub.
